Our Story

A family-owned spot, taking care of your laundry like our own.

Highland Laundromat has served Malden families for years right at the corner of Highland Ave. We’re a small, family-run business — when you come in, you’re talking to the owners.

We’ve recently invested in our space with brand new LG commercial washers and a full lineup of brand new Dexter stack dryers, so your loads come out cleaner, faster, and with less hassle. The shop is bright, the floor is clean, and there’s always someone friendly nearby if you need a hand.

Whether you’re running through a quick load or dropping off the whole week’s laundry, we’re glad to see you.
